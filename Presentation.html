The Framework

How I Manage Revenue

A simplified, high-level look at how I actually work, day to day — and I'm always refining it.

Segmentation & Mapping

Knowing exactly who sends, from where, and through which channel — proven with data, not assumed.

Qualitative Data: Where people send and how — the shops and hubs they use, cash-to-agent habits, the support they need, and the cultural calendar that drives the peaks.

Quantitative Data: Transaction history, historical yield, CAC, LTV, channel, payment method, payout city, send frequency, retention. The conversations tell me what to test; the data tells me whether I'm right.

Pricing Methodology

Pricing each corridor on its own economics — yield, elasticity, and the margin underneath.

Cost-to-serve, decomposed: contribution margin across TB1 → TB2 → TB3 — FX, card-acquiring (PSP) fees, partner payout commissions, and platform cost — so every corridor pays its way before I price it.

The work, not the shortcut: unit economics is where I start, not where I finish. I research both sides, test on a low-risk slice, then align marketing and sales behind the price before it ships.

Predictive Forecasting

Rolling 18-month forecasts that correct themselves as actuals come in — held at 85–95% accuracy.

By segment: forecast by corridor, channel, payer and season, with holiday and salary-cycle peaks built in.

Wired to price: the forecast recalculates the moment a fee or FX markup changes — so I see how a price move ripples through the P&L before I commit. A forecast I trust lets me price with conviction instead of caution.

Competition Mapping

Watching where rivals are slow or stuck — payout delays, compliance holds, thin support — and pricing to take that volume.

Pricing into weakness: I track competitor rates and service continuously. Where a rival has a payout delay or a compliance hold, I place a sharper rate and win the share that's genuinely in play.

Bringing Teams In Early

Pricing can't live in a silo, so I bring product, marketing and sales in from the start.

In from the start: I involve product, marketing and sales early — agreeing the right value, the right channel to reach the customer, and the right way to sell it. Aligned early, everyone moves faster and does more.

Better Rails

Finding cheaper, faster rails so the margin's protected before pricing even starts.

Cheaper settlement: I test alternative settlement rails — newer payment infrastructure included — to compress the correspondent-bank costs that sit between send and payout.

Dual-Force Segmentation

True corridor optimization requires looking past flat geographic data. I map corridors by combining granular numerical distributions (quantitative) with real-world community behavior metrics (qualitative).

Quantitative Layer

Auditing month-over-month transaction frequency, average transaction volume drop-off limits, and localized CAC payback windows.

Deep Dive 2

The Pricing Ritual

A structured, clinical routine designed to eliminate margin leakage and protect profitability at the transaction level. No guesswork, just rigorous financial control:

1.

Cost-to-Serve Decomposition

Decomposing all variables—including PSP acquiring card fees, correspondent payment routing charges, and agent commissions—to define the exact break-even point.

2.

Elasticity Calibration

Analyzing competitor price structures to identify sweet spots where raising transaction spreads or margins captures premium return without inducing transaction churn.

3.

Dynamic Guardrails

Establishing fixed pricing floors that lock out uneconomic transaction tiers and keep the contribution margin positive.
